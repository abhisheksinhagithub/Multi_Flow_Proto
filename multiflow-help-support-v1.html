Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers
Open WhatsApp → Phone Number and follow the connection and verification steps. Once connected, the number status and quality information appear in the dashboard.
Open WhatsApp → Templates, select the rejected template and review its status details. Update the content according to the applicable template requirements and resubmit it.
Go to Billing → Recharge, select a package, review the amount and proceed through the payment flow.
Open Automation → Execution Logs, locate the failed execution, review the error details and use Retry when the issue has been resolved.
Go to Billing → Invoices. Open an invoice to view its billing details and use the PDF action to download it.
Go to Team → Members and use Invite Team Member. You can assign the appropriate role and team during the invitation flow.
