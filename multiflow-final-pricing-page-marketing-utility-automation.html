Pricing FAQ

Clear answers before checkout.

Are Marketing and Utility separate pricing plans?

Yes. Multiflow displays separate WhatsApp Marketing and WhatsApp Utility message-credit pricing.

Is WhatsApp messaging subscription-based?

The message packs on this pricing page are presented as one-time purchases. Automation is the recurring monthly subscription.

What happens when I buy WhatsApp + Automation?

The customer selects Marketing or Utility, chooses a message volume, then chooses an Automation plan. The first-month total is the message pack plus one month of automation.

Does the message pack renew automatically?

No. The intended structure is that message credits are purchased when required. Automation renews monthly according to the selected subscription.

Can I use Utility with Automation?

Yes. The combined section lets the customer choose Utility pricing and then select the Automation plan.

Can I use Marketing with Automation?

Yes. The same combined pricing flow supports Marketing message packs with any applicable Automation plan.

Can I upgrade my Automation plan?

Yes. Customers can move between Starter, Growth, Pro and Enterprise as their workflow requirements change.

Are taxes and Meta charges included?

The prices shown here are the base Multiflow pricing structure. Applicable GST/taxes and any Meta/WhatsApp charges should be shown separately during checkout.

Need custom pricing?

For enterprise volume, reseller requirements, multiple numbers or custom automation.
